How the battle works +

Closest target. Trainers and companions aim for the closest living enemy. Place your knight at the front to meet approaching enemies. Any party members can share a formation row.

Movement matters. Melee units close to 12 arena units; Bloomslime attacks at 27; casters at 34. Stonehorn moves slowly, Emberfox quickly. A ready attack waits for range without spending its cooldown. Slow reduces movement and action speed by 40%; Springstep boosts both by 30%. Cooldowns count down normally. Units steer around each other; circular obstacle collision is supported by the simulation.

Explicit exceptions. Skyneedle and Crown Hex can pursue trainers while monsters live, but still need range. Skyneedle reaches 46 units. Wild Lunge pursues the closest enemy with 18-unit reach. Arc Nova can hit trainers within its casting range. Chain Spark only hits monsters in range until none remain. Bondguard redirects 60% of trainer damage to the living tank anywhere in the arena.

Support and priority. Support spells work across the whole friendly team. Heals and shields choose the living ally with the lowest health percentage. Heals wait for 25 missing HP; cleanse waits for a debuff or 30 missing HP. The first ready, useful skill takes priority: an out-of-range attack is pursued, not skipped. Otherwise, use a basic attack.

Predictable combat. Physical accuracy uses attacker level and DEX against defender level and AGI. Combat uses a saved encounter seed; there are no critical hits. The same build produces the same result on every screen size. At 55 seconds, healing stops and damage doubles; at 75 seconds, trainer health percentage breaks a tie. Monster passives apply automatically. Skill trees add persistent bonuses to your party. Wild packs and the boss have no trainer: defeat every enemy before the time limit, or lose. Direct-trainer skills target a monster when no enemy trainer exists. Healing bonuses do not apply in Overcharge. Druid fusion and online group combat are outside this local prototype.
