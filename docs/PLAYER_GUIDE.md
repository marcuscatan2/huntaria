# Player guide

Open [the game](http://127.0.0.1:8765/) while the local server is running.
Bookmark that address with Ctrl+D. [Operations](../features/delivery/OPERATIONS.md)
explains how to restart the server.

## Begin an adventure

Create a named Apprentice with a dagger or bow. Click to move or approach a
monster. Firstlight residents wait for you to begin a hunt. Roaming monsters on
other maps attack when you approach, even if you are much stronger.

Follow the small quest tracker. Defeat a Brimble, open **Bag**, select its first
guaranteed Soul Echo and summon it. Your first companion joins the party
automatically. The Forest Mage asks for a second companion; summon a Bloomslime
or Stonehorn and win the Mage's proof to open Firstlight's roads.

Yellow `!` and `?` markers identify quest offers and ready deliveries in the
field and minimap. The Atlas shows destinations and roads; movement follows
physical gates. Map danger colors compare wildlife levels with your trainer.

## Battles and recovery

Battles run automatically. Choose your party, skills and formation between
encounters. Fights continue while you browse other screens; nearby monsters can
join. **Run** starts a three-second retreat. Survive it to escape with injuries
and earned drops. Loot notifications disappear, but collected items remain in Bag.

Wounds persist after ordinary victories. Firstlight's camp offers free rest;
arriving in a city heals your entire collection. Defeat returns you to camp or
a rescue city. City shops sell portable recovery supplies.

## Classes and the ghost tower

Continue through the class demonstrations and Tidecrown, then defeat a class
master. At level 20, choose Druid, Mage, Hunter or Swordsman. Your master joins
the ensuing raid and rescues the party, then asks you to hunt for Echoes.

Follow the tracker through the Echo delivery and ghost tower. Keep Casketot in
your active party when speaking to Tully in the rooftop cemetery. Return his
clue to your master to receive a class weapon in Bag and finish the current main
quest. The farm opens at level 25. Each companion has its own skill tree from
the moment you summon it, with bonus points after Tidecrown and the relic quest.

## Companions and menus

Bag holds Inventory. Inner Sea groups Homestead under Sea land; attributes,
Class Skill Tree and Equipment under Trainer; and My companions, Formation and
Species Guide under Party. Use Formation to substitute companions or run a Dummy
test. Mastery tree is above each companion's portrait; its held item appears
below the level. Red dots lead to available upgrades and compatible held items.

New companions start with one signature and two basic moves, then learn more
through level 48. Signature moves are violet. Every city's Move tutor teaches
General moves to active companions. Reset talents refunds a companion's talents
for one matching Echo. An owned species cannot be summoned again. Choosing a
class refunds the trainer's spent attributes.

## Testing without resetting normal progress

[Isolated QA](http://127.0.0.1:8765/?test=1) has a separate save and visible
restart, healing and speed controls. Use that address for a fresh playthrough.
The [review walkthrough](../features/opening/VALIDATION.md) describes what to try.
