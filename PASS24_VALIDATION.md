# Patch 24 owner validation — the Apprentice road

Use the isolated test build at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/?test=1`, then choose
**Restart progress**. Play at normal speed. This review is about discovery,
combat identity and pacing; the automated suite has already checked receipts,
save migration and route reachability.

## Session 1 — first 30 minutes

1. Create a named Apprentice with the dagger. Orient yourself, move around and
   let the designated Emberfox make contact. Confirm the attack feels readable
   and fair without a tutorial conversation.
2. Defeat it. Confirm the Soul Echo appears as a three-second field pickup, then
   follow the single Journey objective into Inventory and summon it. Emberfox
   should occupy the first open companion slot automatically.
3. Continue until the two physical trail signs introduce Bloomslime and
   Stonehorn. Pick either habitat through movement and combat, not a selection
   window. Confirm the sign explains the useful difference without exposing
   drop rates or backend rules.
4. Summon the earned second companion. Fight Tavi with the resulting full party.
   Confirm her Druid sustain is visible and that changing formation or ability
   order feels meaningful.
5. Record elapsed time, deaths, recovery trips, confusing pauses and whether you
   wanted to continue. Repeat later with the bow and the other second companion.

Accept this session when both weapons feel viable, the two roles feel like real
choices, defeat never strands progress, and no guide chain is needed to find the
next useful action.

## Session 2 — class readability and first boss

1. Follow the one current objective through Rain, Lina and Wren. After each
   battle, say what the opposing trainer did without consulting its skill list.
   The four expected ideas are Druid sustain, Mage control, Druid area control
   and Mage trainer-bypass pressure.
2. Enter Springwater Cave and fight fixed-level Tidecrown. Confirm the warning,
   impact and recovery window connect visually. Change formation, ward or guard
   in response rather than winning only through levels.
3. Confirm the Druid and Mage masters become visible after the accepted boss win.

Accept when the two classes read differently through play and Tidecrown teaches
one preparation response without a manual dodge or new combat resource.

## Session 3 — choice, adaptation and level 30

1. Try both master encounters before committing. Choose three of each class's
   five skills and confirm the trial uses your real companions without changing
   the saved Apprentice build.
2. At player level 20, commit to Druid or Mage. Reload immediately. Confirm the
   class, party, Inventory, appearance, position and completed route remain.
3. Complete the application, counter-building and ability-proof encounters.
   Replace one equipped monster skill in **Party & bag** and confirm the proof
   requires that real build change.
4. Continue through the three Amber challenges and fixed Amber guardian. At
   player level 30, spend one point in one individual monster tree and win the
   final proof. A duplicate of that species must retain its own untouched tree.

Accept when the class commitment is understandable, the new class immediately
feels useful, the counter loop rewards a comprehensible adjustment, and the
species tree clearly affects a named skill or innate identity.

## Decisions this review does not silently make

- Whether class commitment should be literally permanent or recoverable through
  a future server-controlled retraining path.
- What bounded, non-paid monster-development use unlocks Inner Sea ownership at
  level 25. That milestone is intentionally absent for now.
- Final numerical balance, ordinary Echo rates, release economy or approval of
  all 100 creature designs.

Reply with the session number and the first moment that felt confusing, unfair
or dull. An approval of this route closes OR-02 for its current revision; it does
not approve final art or later-region production.
